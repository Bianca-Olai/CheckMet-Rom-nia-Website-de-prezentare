Meet Our Coaches

Adrian Rosu

Professor

Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

About Adrian

Adrian Roșu is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu coach and black belt with over 12 years of experience practicing the sport. A graduate of the Master's degree program in Physical Education and Sport at “Aurel Vlaicu” University of Arad, Adrian combines his experience on the mats with an academic background in sports and physical education.

A multiple medalist at Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu competitions in Romania and abroad, Adrian emphasizes discipline, technique, continuous development and building strong character through sport.

At CHECKMAT Romania, his dedication is focused especially on the development of children and young people, providing a safe, disciplined and friendly environment where every athlete is encouraged to progress at their own pace.

For Adrian, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is about more than techniques and competitions — it is a way for children to learn respect, discipline, self-confidence and the importance of consistent effort.

Diana Angela Hetco

Instructor

Blue Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

About Diana

Diana Angela Hetco is an instructor for the children's group at CHECKMAT Romania, passionate about sports and dedicated to the development of the youngest Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu practitioners.

She is nationally certified as a Sports Instructor in fitness and has extensive competition experience, having won multiple medals at Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu competitions both in Romania and abroad.

Through patience, energy and individual attention to every child, Diana helps create an environment where children can learn Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in a safe, disciplined and enjoyable way. For her, every training session is an opportunity to help children develop not only their athletic skills, but also their confidence, respect, discipline and team spirit.

Mihai Matei

Professor

Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

About Mihai

Mihai Matei is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu coach and black belt with over 12 years of experience practicing the sport and 10 years of coaching experience. A student at the Faculty of Physical Education and Sport at “Aurel Vlaicu” University of Arad, Mihai complements his practical experience on the mats with academic studies in the field of sports.

A multiple medalist at Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu competitions both in Romania and abroad, Mihai is dedicated to continuous development, both as an athlete and as a coach.

At CHECKMAT Romania, he emphasizes technique, discipline, attention to detail and the individual progress of every practitioner. His years of experience allow him to adapt the training process to each athlete's level, goals and individual pace.

For Mihai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is not only about competition or performance, but a continuous process of learning, development and pushing beyond one's own limits.
